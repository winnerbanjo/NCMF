Bukola Thomas is a Nollywood actor, model, voice-over artist, and
entrepreneur whose career reflects a blend of beauty, intellect, and
talent. Born to a Sierra Leonean mother and a father from Lagos Island, she
proudly identifies as a Lagos indigene. She holds a bachelor’s degree in
Religion from Lagos State University, as well as an MBA in Marketing,
academic achievements that complement her eloquence, sharp diction, and
strong interpersonal skills.

Before embracing acting full-time, Bukola built her early career in
marketing with Scoa Nigeria Plc and Three Fold Nigeria Ltd. In 2013, she
made a successful transition into Nollywood, where her distinctive carriage
and authenticity quickly distinguished her. Over the years, she has
expanded her reach beyond film, excelling as a model and voice-over artist
while also pursuing entrepreneurial ventures that reflect her drive and
creativity.

She is admired not only for her craft but also for her amiable personality,
wit, and compassion. A devoted Christian and perfectionist at heart, she
approaches every role with discipline and passion, bringing authenticity
and depth to her performances. With her unique blend of talent and
professionalism, she continues to grow as an artist and entrepreneur,
building a career that resonates both within Nollywood and across the wider
creative industry
