Ijeoma Richards — Award-Winning Actor, Producer & Filmmaker | President,
NCMF | CEO, TRIMM Network

Ijeoma Richards is an award-winning actor, film producer, and creative
executive. She holds an MBA from the University of Lagos, combining
artistic excellence with strong business and leadership acumen. She studied
film directing with Kinabuti in 2014 and film production at Del-York
Creative Academy.
With verified IMDb credits, she has appeared in over 100 films and
television series, including Rave, Husbands of Lagos, Aunty Maria, Make Me
a Girl Again, Newman Street, and Salome. Known for her versatility and
depth, she moves effortlessly between English, Igbo, and Yoruba
productions, as well as drama, comedy, and character roles.

Business & Leadership

She is the CEO of TRIMM Network, a media, event consultancy, and celebrity
management firm through which she produces original work. Her production
credits include the popular online series #AdukeSeries (Adukefoodspot), the
critically acclaimed documentary Uru Igba-Boi on the Igbo apprenticeship
system, and feature films such as Grandma’s Neighbours, The Deputy, and
Comfort My Love.
She is the Co-Founder and President of the Nollywood Creative Minds Forum
(NCMF), where in 2024 she received a Surprise Leadership Excellence Award
from members for her outstanding stewardship. Under her leadership, NCMF
has won multiple industry awards and produced acclaimed projects including
the DStv hit feature film Perfect for Divorce.

She is the founder of the biennial Film Industry Legacy Award. Her work
across the screen and boardroom has earned her the Actors Guild of Nigeria
(AGN) Merit Award (2017), Best Supporting Actress at the Nigeria Women
Achievers Awards, and Entertainment Personality of the Year at the 2022
Nigeria Women Achievers Awards.
