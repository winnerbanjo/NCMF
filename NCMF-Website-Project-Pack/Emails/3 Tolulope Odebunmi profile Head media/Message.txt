TOLULOPE ODEBUNMI

Filmmaker | Director | Writer | Producer | Actor | Creative Entrepreneur

One-Stop Filmmaker. Storyteller. Creative Visionary.

Tolulope Odebunmi is a Nigerian filmmaker, screen and stage actor, writer,
director, producer and creative entrepreneur passionate about using
storytelling as a catalyst for entertainment, inspiration and social change.

He is the Founder and Creative Lead of JISYEM'S VIEW PRODUCTIONS, an
independent creative production company committed to developing and
producing authentic African stories with universal appeal. Through the
company, he explores the intersection of film and television production,
theatre, creative technology, advertising, events and entertainment, with a
vision of building a commercially sustainable African creative enterprise
for the global market.

As a storyteller, Tolulope believes that stories should do more than
entertain; they should challenge perspectives, inspire conversations and
leave a positive impact on audiences. His creative philosophy is rooted in
the belief that African stories deserve authentic representation,
compelling execution and a place on the global stage.

FILMMAKING & CREATIVE EXPERIENCE

Tolulope's creative journey spans filmmaking, theatre, acting, writing,
directing, producing, media management and creative development. He has
worked across different aspects of production, from conceptualisation and
script development to directing, production management, performance and
audience engagement.

He is particularly interested in character-driven narratives, emotionally
engaging stories, social-impact films, African cultural experiences and
innovative storytelling formats, including vertical episodic dramas and
digital content.

His professional development includes participation in the British Council
FilmLab Africa, a competitive filmmaking programme that brought together
emerging African filmmakers for intensive training, mentorship and creative
development. Through the programme, he strengthened his approach to
screenwriting, directing and film production, working with industry mentors
including Abbesi Akhamie and Oladipo O. Fresh.

He is also experienced in production planning and industry-standard
creative tools, including Final Draft and Movie Magic.

ACTING & SCREEN CREDITS

Tolulope began his acting journey in 2016 and has since developed
experience across screen and stage performances. A student and practitioner
of Method Acting, he approaches performance with an emphasis on character
psychology, emotional authenticity and truthful storytelling.

His screen credits include:

- King of Boys 2 – Taofik
- Africa Magic Brethren – Lagos Shepherd
- The Origin: Madam Koikoi – Ige
- Africa Magic Itura – Ijakumo
- Africa Magic Battleground – Tintin
- Bottled Up – Director and Producer

He continues to explore opportunities across film, television, streaming
platforms, theatre and international co-productions.

SELECTED FILM & THEATRE PROJECTS

BOTTLED UP

A British Council FilmLab Africa-supported short film directed and produced
by Tolulope. The project explores the power of cinematic storytelling and
has received recognition on the festival circuit, including Best Short Film
at the Kaduna International Film Festival. It has also received recognition
at An Evening of Short Film Festival and was nominated for screening at the
Bayelsa International Film Festival.

MENTALITY: WHAT MEN DON'T SAY

An original theatrical production written by Tolulope Odebunmi and
presented by JISYEM'S VIEW PRODUCTIONS. The play explores masculinity,
emotional vulnerability, grief, depression, relationships, addiction and
the conversations men often struggle to have. Selected for the Lagos Fringe
Festival, the production reflects his commitment to socially relevant
storytelling and creating spaces for meaningful conversations.

THE GRACE WE SHARE

A family comedy-drama developed for vertical episodic storytelling,
reflecting his interest in contemporary African family experiences, faith,
relationships and the transformative power of grace.

His other creative development interests include feature films, short
films, limited series, vertical dramas, stage productions, advertising
campaigns and socially conscious entertainment properties.

LEADERSHIP & MEDIA

Tolulope serves as the Head of Media at NCMF, where he brings his creative,
communication and production experience to media leadership and
storytelling.

His professional experience also includes serving as a Head of Media
Department and in-house producer, overseeing creative teams and
productions, including stage productions and feature-film projects.

He is passionate about building creative teams, developing emerging talent,
creating effective media strategies and using visual communication to
connect brands, stories and audiences.

THE VISION

Through his work, Tolulope Odebunmi continues to pursue a vision of
creating stories that entertain, inspire and make a lasting difference.

He is committed to telling authentic African stories, developing
commercially viable creative properties, collaborating with exceptional
talents and building bridges between African creativity and the
international entertainment industry.

Whether behind the camera, on stage, in front of the camera or leading a
creative team, his mission remains the same: to tell stories that matter.
